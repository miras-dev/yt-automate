# Sources

Checked 2026-11-10 (winter semester 2026/27). Every claim in the voiceover has a primary source and a cross-check.

## Scene 1 - Zero euros

- **Most public universities charge no tuition, even for students from abroad**
  - DAAD, Costs of education and living: "State universities in Germany charge no tuition fees for a first degree"
    (exceptions listed): https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/living-in-germany/finances/
  - Cross-check: GradGermany, Germany tuition fees by state 2026 (all 16 states):
    https://www.gradgermany.com/blog/germany-tuition-fees-by-state-2026
- **The big exceptions: Baden-Württemberg and the Technical University of Munich charge students from outside the EU**
  - DAAD (same page): Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU citizens EUR 1,500 per semester; in Bavaria some universities may
    charge non-EU students, e.g. TUM since winter semester 2024/25.
  - Cross-check: Hochschule Esslingen, Financing your studies (EUR 1,500 per semester for non-EU/EEA students since
    WS 2017/18): https://hs-esslingen.de/en/study/advice-support/financing
- **Everyone pays a semester fee instead, usually a few hundred euros**
  - DAAD (same page): the semester contribution "varies between higher education institutions and comes to between 70 and
    430 EUR"; it covers student services and student government.
  - Cross-check: University of Duisburg-Essen sheet citing DAAD (100-350 EUR):
    https://www.io.msm.uni-due.de/fileadmin/Dateien/IO/Infos_fuer_Partner_durch_MSM/Student_Contribution_Fee.pdf
- **At many universities, that fee includes the ticket (scene 2)**
  - UDE sheet (above): "at many higher education institutions also includes a semester ticket".
  - Cross-check: Expatrio, Semester ticket in Germany: https://www.expatrio.com/about-germany/semester-ticket-germany

## Scene 2 - The ticket

- **The Deutschland-Semesterticket covers buses, trams, subways and regional trains all over Germany (not long-distance trains)**
  - Bundesregierung, Deutschlandticket FAQ (valid nationwide on local and regional transport; IC/EC/ICE excluded):
    https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/deutschlandticket-2134074
  - Cross-check: Deutsche Bahn, In welchen Zügen gilt das Deutschland-Ticket? (RB, RE, S-Bahn, buses, trams, U-Bahn):
    https://www.bahn.de/faq/deutschlandticket-verkehrsmittel-deutschland
- **This semester it costs 37.80 euros a month, 60 % of the regular Deutschlandticket**
  - Deutschlandticket EUR 63 a month since January 2026 (Bundesregierung, above; bahn.de
    https://www.bahn.de/angebot/regio/deutschland-ticket). 60 % of 63 = 37.80, valid from winter semester 2026/27.
  - Cross-check: ticket-rechner.de, Deutschlandticket für Studenten 2026 (37.80 from WS 2026/27; 34.80 until SS 2026):
    https://ticket-rechner.de/ratgeber/deutschlandticket-studenten/ ; Expatrio (226.80 per semester).
- **Cologne to Berlin on regional trains for no extra cost, just much slower** - follows from the validity above
  (regional trains only); an illustration, not a timetable claim.

## Scene 3 - The academic quarter

- **c.t. = cum tempore: a lecture at 8 c.t. starts at 8:15 (the academic quarter); s.t. = sine tempore, on time**
  - University of Hohenheim, The term c.t. / s.t.: https://uni-hohenheim.de/term-ct-st
  - Cross-check: University of Münster, Small University ABC (c.t. is the general rule):
    https://www.uni-muenster.de/Geschichte/histsem/EnglishPages/abc.shtml ; Wikipedia, Academic quarter (class timing).
- **At the end of a lecture students knock on their desks instead of clapping; nobody is sure how it started**
  - DAAD study-in-germany.de, Things you might not know yet about universities in Germany (part 2):
    https://www.study-in-germany.de/en/community/things-you-might-not-know-yet-about-universities-in-germany-part-2
  - Cross-check: IamExpat, Why do German students knock on their desks? (competing origin theories; "nobody really knows"):
    https://www.iamexpat.de/education/education-news/most-googled-why-do-german-students-knock-their-desks

## Scene 4 - Mensa and a job

- **The Mensa is run by the Studierendenwerk**
  - Deutsches Studierendenwerk, Mensa - warum sie politisch so wichtig ist:
    https://www.studierendenwerke.de/beitrag/mensa-warum-sie-politisch-so-wichtig-ist
  - Cross-check: Studentenwerk Dresden menu pages (student prices) https://www.studentenwerk-dresden.de/english/mensen/speiseplan/mensa-johanna.html
- **A hot main dish often costs less than four euros; in Heidelberg it averages about 2.90**
  - Heidelberg24 on the voy Mensa ranking: average main dish EUR 2.90 in Heidelberg, EUR 4.71 in Hamburg, EUR 3.66 across all
    compared cities: https://www.heidelberg24.de/heidelberg/mensa-ranking-essen-preise-vergleich-studierende-guenstig-94485257.html
  - Cross-check: Studentenwerk Dresden 2026 menus (student prices EUR 2.18-3.57); tageskarte.io, Mensa-Preise schwanken stark:
    https://www.tageskarte.io/gastronomie/detail/mensa-preise-schwanken-stark.html
- **During the semester students can work up to 20 hours a week (and keep their student status)**
  - Deutsches Studierendenwerk, Jobben flyer 2026: https://www.studierendenwerke.de/fileadmin/user_upload/260120_DSW_Flyer_Jobbing_web.pdf
  - Cross-check: studierenplus.de, Werkstudent: https://www.studierenplus.de/bildung-finanzieren/werkstudent
- **Minimum wage 13.90 euros an hour (2026)**
  - Bundesregierung, Mindestlohn jetzt bei 13,90 Euro: https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/schwerpunkte/wirksam-regieren/mindestlohn-steigt-2391010
  - Cross-check: Minijob-Zentrale, Mindestlohn für Studierende: https://magazin.minijob-zentrale.de/mindestlohn-studierende-minijob/
- **Students from outside the EU are limited to 140 full days (or 280 half days) a year**
  - DSW Jobben flyer 2026 (above).
  - Cross-check: GradGermany, Student work limit Germany 2026: https://www.gradgermany.com/blog/work-limit-revised-for-international-students-in-germany-2026

## Scene 5 - Rent and Pfand

- **A WG room averages 512 euros a month, about 800 in Munich**
  - Studis Online on the Moses Mendelssohn Institut / wg-gesucht.de study (March 2026): average EUR 512; Munich EUR 800:
    https://www.studis-online.de/studienkosten/wg-zimmer-mietspiegel.php
  - Cross-check: MLP Studentenwohnreport 2026 (Munich model WG room about EUR 850):
    https://mlp-se.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/2026/mlp-studentenwohnreport-2026/ ; t-online München (EUR 800).
- **Every single-use plastic bottle and can carries a 25-cent deposit (Pfand), and shops have to take them back**
  - Verbraucherzentrale, Fragen und Antworten zum Einwegpfand (always 25 cents; retailers must take back and refund):
    https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/wissen/umwelt-haushalt/abfall/fragen-und-antworten-alles-zum-einwegpfand-und-dosenpfand-11505
  - Cross-check: Hanseatic Bank, Pfand auf Flaschen und Dosen:
    https://www.hanseaticbank.de/klarmacher/bezahlen/pfand-auf-flaschen-und-dosen-wie-viel-gibt-es-fuer-leergut-und-wo
- **On Sundays almost every supermarket in Germany is closed by law**
  - Library of Congress, Shop closing laws in Germany (Ladenschlussgesetz; exceptions at major stations, airports, petrol
    stations): https://blogs.loc.gov/law/2025/11/shop-closing-laws-in-germany/
  - Cross-check: Berlin.de, Shopping hours and Sunday shopping:
    https://www.berlin.de/en/tourism/travel-information/1740536-2862820-ladenoeffnungszeiten-sonntagsverkauf.en.html

## Scene 6 - The real bill

- **To get a student visa, students from outside the EU must show 11,904 euros for one year (992 a month), often in a blocked account**
  - Auswärtiges Amt (German embassy pages): "992,-- EUR pro Monat, d.h. für die Dauer von einem Jahr mindestens 11.904,-- EUR";
    a blocked account is the usual proof: https://maskat.diplo.de/om-de/service/05-visaeinreise/2164012-2164012 ,
    https://quito.diplo.de/ec-de/service/05-visaeinreise/2433880-2433880
  - Cross-check: DAAD costs page (above): "as of 1 January 2025, you must prove that you have 992 euros per month".
- **Tuition isn't part of that number** - Auswärtiges Amt pages: if the university charges fees, they must be shown in
  addition to the 11,904 euros.
- **An average WG room would eat more than half of it** - arithmetic from the sources above: 512 / 992 = 52 %.
- **"Studying is free. Sleeping isn't."** - the video's conclusion from the facts above (no tuition at most public
  universities; rent is the largest single cost in the example); not a separate factual claim.
