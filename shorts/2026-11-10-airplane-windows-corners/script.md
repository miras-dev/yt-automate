# Why don't airplane windows have corners?

**Idea:** Airplane windows are rounded because square windows on the first jet airliner, the de Havilland Comet, cracked at the corners and tore the plane open in 1954.

## Voiceover

Look at any plane window. It has no corners.
That shape isn't a design choice.
It's a fix for a disaster.
The first jet airliner, the Comet, had square windows.
Every flight, the cabin pressurised and relaxed.
Tiny cracks grew at the corners.
In 1954, two of them tore open mid-flight.
Round windows spread that stress evenly.
So no corner ever gets the chance to rip the plane apart.
